EU AI Act Compliance Checker
EU AI Act Compliance Checker
The official AI Act Compliance Checker helps users understand which AI Act rules may apply to their AI system, including possible obligations for providers, deployers and other operators under Regulation (EU) 2024/1689.
Currently in its beta version, the Compliance Checker represents an ongoing project, and we welcome your participation in its development. Your feedback and insights are important to refining this tool. Please send your feedback to the AI Office (CNECT-AIOFFICE@ec.europa.eu).
